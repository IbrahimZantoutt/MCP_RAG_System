Classification: Internal


ABOUT THIS HANDBOOK

Helix Robotics is 412 people across Austin (headquarters), Reno (field
operations hub), and Rotterdam (EMEA). We build autonomous mobile robots for
warehouses. This handbook covers employment terms, benefits, leave, and conduct.

It is not a contract and does not create one. Where this handbook conflicts with
your offer letter or with local law, those prevail. Where it conflicts with the
Safety Standards and Certification policy on any safety matter, that policy
prevails without exception.


EMPLOYMENT BASICS

Working hours
  Standard is 40 hours per week. Most roles have flexible start and finish times.
  Manufacturing, field operations, and support operate on defined shifts, since
  customer facilities and production lines do not flex.

Remote and hybrid work
  Role-dependent, and we are honest that this is not equal across the company.

    Fully remote eligible      Software engineering (Fleet Software, Platform),
                               Sales, Marketing, Finance, People Ops
    Hybrid, 3 days on site     Firmware, Perception, Quality — these roles need
                               regular access to the hardware-in-the-loop rigs
                               and the test bay
    On site                    Manufacturing, Supply Chain
    Field-based                Field Operations, Field Technicians

  Managers set team expectations within these bands. Fully remote employees are
  expected on site for two company gatherings per year, with travel covered.

Location changes
  Moving to a different country, or to a US state where Helix has no employment
  presence, requires People Ops approval and is not always possible. Discuss
  before committing to a move.


COMPENSATION

Review cycle
  Annual, effective 1 April, with market adjustments made off-cycle where
  benchmarking shows a genuine gap. Engineering compensation bands are published
  internally and are described in the Hiring and Leveling Guide.

Equity
  All employees receive equity at hire. Four-year vesting, one-year cliff,
  monthly thereafter. Refresh grants at the annual review for employees beyond
  their second year.

Bonus
  Company performance bonus, target 8 percent of base for individual
  contributors and 12 to 20 percent for directors and above. Paid annually in
  March based on the prior fiscal year. The 2024 bonus pool was funded at 82
  percent of target, reflecting revenue ahead of plan and gross margin below it.

On-call compensation
  On-call is compensated separately and is not part of base expectations. Rates,
  eligibility, and the provisions for extended incident work are in the On-Call
  Compensation Policy. Read it before your first rotation — people routinely
  fail to claim what they are owed for long incidents.


TIME OFF

Paid time off
  Salaried employees receive 25 days per year, accrued monthly. Shift-based
  employees receive 22 days plus shift differential arrangements.

  Carryover is capped at 5 days into the following year. We do not pay out
  accrued PTO except where local law requires it.

  We would rather you take it. Average utilization in 2024 was 17.4 days, which
  is too low, and managers are asked to actively encourage time off rather than
  merely approve it.

Public holidays
  Per work location. US: 11 days. Netherlands: per Dutch statutory calendar.
  Employees may substitute a public holiday for a day of personal or religious
  significance by notifying their manager.

Sick leave
  15 days per year, not accrued, no medical certificate required for absences
  under 3 consecutive days. Longer absences follow local statutory processes.

  Do not come to work sick. This is stated with particular force for anyone
  working in manufacturing, the test bay, or on a customer floor — an impaired
  person in a robot operating area is a safety matter, not just a health one.

Parental leave
  Primary caregiver: 18 weeks fully paid. Secondary caregiver: 8 weeks fully
  paid. Available from day one of employment, with no qualifying period.
  Adoption and surrogacy are covered identically. Phased return over 4 weeks at
  full pay is available on request.

Bereavement leave
  10 days for an immediate family member, 3 days otherwise. Extensions are
  granted routinely; ask your manager, who is instructed to say yes.

Unpaid leave and sabbatical
  Unpaid leave up to 3 months is available with manager and People Ops approval.
  Employees reaching 5 years of service are eligible for a 4-week paid
  sabbatical, in addition to PTO.


BENEFITS

Health
  US: medical, dental, and vision from day one. Helix pays 100 percent of the
  employee premium and 80 percent of dependent premiums. Netherlands: statutory
  coverage plus a supplementary allowance.

Mental health
  Six funded therapy or counselling sessions per year through the employee
  assistance programme, plus unlimited access to the associated digital
  services. Usage is confidential and Helix receives only aggregate numbers.

Retirement
  US 401(k) with 5 percent company match, immediately vested. Netherlands:
  pension per the applicable arrangement.

Home office
  1,200 USD equipment allowance at hire, refreshed at 600 USD every two years.
  Covers desk, chair, monitor, and peripherals. Equipment remains Helix property
  above 500 USD in value and is returned on departure. Home office equipment is
  handled by People Ops, not through the expense policy.

Learning
  2,500 USD per person per year for courses, conferences, books, and
  certifications. Manager approval only. Conference travel comes out of the
  travel budget rather than this allowance.

Parking and commuting
  Austin and Reno: free on-site parking. Rotterdam: public transport pass or
  cycling allowance.


CONDUCT AND SAFETY

Safety obligations
  Every employee, regardless of role, is subject to the following without
  exception:

    - Anyone entering a robot operating area at any Helix or customer facility
      must have completed the floor entry protocol briefing. This includes
      executives and visitors. There is no exemption for seniority or for being
      in a hurry.
    - All engineering staff complete a two-hour safety orientation in week one,
      including those who will never touch firmware. It is required for Austin
      test bay access.
    - Alcohol is not permitted at any Helix facility with a robot test bay, on
      any customer site, or during an on-call shift. This is a safety policy.
    - Any near-miss, any contact between a robot and a person however minor, and
      any unexpected robot motion must be reported to Safety and Compliance
      within 24 hours. Reporting a near-miss is never held against the reporter,
      and any manager who suggests otherwise is in breach of this handbook.

Anti-harassment and discrimination
  Helix does not tolerate harassment or discrimination on any basis. Reports go
  to your manager, People Ops, or the anonymous reporting line. All reports are
  investigated. Retaliation against a reporter is itself grounds for termination.

Confidentiality
  Much of what we do is confidential: customer deployments, pricing, roadmap,
  bill-of-materials data, and incident detail. Customer names may be used
  externally only where a public reference agreement exists.

  Specific and frequently misunderstood: internal postmortems are internal.
  Customer-facing incident summaries are prepared separately and are approved by
  the account team. Do not forward an internal postmortem to a customer, however
  well intentioned.

Use of customer data
  Covered fully in the Data Handling and Privacy Policy. In brief: access
  customer data only with a recorded reason, never store diagnostic captures on
  personal machines, and never attach them to email.

Outside work
  Permitted where it does not compete with Helix, does not use Helix resources,
  and does not impair your work. Disclose to your manager. Anything involving
  autonomous mobile robots, warehouse automation, or a Helix customer requires
  People Ops approval.


TRAVEL AND EXPENSES

Covered by the Travel and Expense Policy. The single most important provision:
during an active SEV-1 or SEV-2 incident, no pre-approval is required for any
travel expense. Book the flight and go; explain afterward. That rule exists
because in September 2024 a field engineer spent hours chasing approval for a
1,400 USD Saturday fare during a critical outage at our largest customer.


PERFORMANCE AND GROWTH

Reviews
  Twice yearly: a full review in March and a lighter check-in in September.
  Ratings feed compensation and promotion decisions.

Promotion
  Engineering levels and expectations are in the Hiring and Leveling Guide.
  Promotion is a recognition that someone is already operating at the next
  level, not a bet that they will grow into it.

Feedback
  We ask for direct feedback given early. The engineering norm of stating what
  you actually checked in a code review rather than a bare approval is a
  specific instance of a general expectation.

Internal mobility
  Employees may apply for any internal role after 12 months in their current
  one, or sooner with manager agreement. Managers are expected not to block
  internal moves.


LEAVING

Notice
  4 weeks for individual contributors, 8 weeks for directors and above, or as
  specified in your contract, or as local law requires — whichever is longer.

Offboarding
  Return of equipment, access revocation, and a knowledge handover. Note that
  Operator Console accounts at customer sites are not covered by centralized
  deprovisioning, since Fleet OS does not yet support single sign-on. Anyone
  with customer-site console accounts must have them removed manually as part of
  offboarding. People Ops maintains the checklist; SSO is on the 2025 roadmap
  and will close this gap.

Exit interviews
  Offered to everyone, conducted by People Ops, and confidential. Aggregate
  themes are reported to the executive team quarterly.

Alumni
  We rehire. Six people have returned since 2021, and the eligibility criteria
  are simply that you left on reasonable terms.
