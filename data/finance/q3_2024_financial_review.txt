EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Q3 2024 was a quarter of solid top-line performance undermined by a single
operational event.

Revenue came in at 22.4 million USD, up 31 percent year over year and modestly
ahead of plan. Annual recurring revenue closed the quarter at 33.2 million USD.
Gross margin was 51 percent, roughly flat sequentially and below where this
business needs to operate. Net loss was 4.1 million USD. Cash and equivalents
stood at 61.0 million USD at quarter end, giving approximately 14 quarters of
runway at the current burn.

The quarter's defining event was incident INC-2024-017, the charge dock
reservation deadlock at Cardinal Foods' Memphis distribution center on
September 14. The direct financial impact was 148,000 USD in service credits
plus approximately 190,000 USD in unplanned engineering and field cost. The
indirect impact — a roughly two-month delay to the Cardinal Foods Atlanta
expansion decision — is larger than both, and is the reason this incident
appears in a financial review rather than only in an engineering document.

GROSS MARGIN

  Hardware gross margin                   34 percent
  Software gross margin                   88 percent
  Services gross margin                   22 percent
  Blended gross margin                    51 percent

Blended gross margin of 51 percent is the central financial problem of this
company and it is almost entirely a hardware bill-of-materials issue. At 34
percent, hardware margin is thin enough that hardware discounting materially
damages company economics, which is why the discount floor is set at 38 percent
blended and why software licence discounting requires my approval and is
essentially never granted.

Two levers are in motion:

  - HX-200 hardware revision D, targeting a 14 percent BOM reduction, scheduled
    H2 2025. Principal savings are drive board integration, a single-vendor
    LiDAR strategy replacing the current dual-source arrangement, and chassis
    part-count reduction. The critical path risk is IEC 61508 SIL 2 re-assessment
    on any change touching the safety scanner chain; engineering has engaged
    Nikolai Petrov and the certification body from the start rather than at the
    end, which is the right sequencing.

  - Helix Vision attach rate, currently 31 percent. Vision is a high-margin
    add-on at 8,900 USD per unit and every point of attach rate improvement flows
    almost directly to gross margin.

Services margin of 22 percent is low but not concerning; services are a
deployment enabler, not a profit center, and site assessments in particular are
credited against orders.

INCIDENT INC-2024-017 — FINANCIAL IMPACT

Recorded here in full because the board asked for it explicitly at the October
meeting.

Direct costs recognized in Q3 2024

  Service credit, Cardinal Foods Memphis            148,000 USD
  Unplanned engineering cost (approx. 90 hours
    remediation plus 8 weeks of partial capacity
    across four teams on postmortem action items)   142,000 USD
  Field operations and travel (on-site executive
    review, emergency deployment support)            31,000 USD
  Legal and account management time                  17,000 USD
  Total direct                                      338,000 USD

On the service credit specifically: the contractual formula figure was 31,800
USD, calculated as 50 percent of the Memphis monthly licence fee of 63,600 USD
(240 units at 265 USD Enterprise). The assessed and paid figure was 148,000 USD.

The difference is a negotiated goodwill credit that Rowan Achebe and I approved
jointly, covering the full quarter's Memphis licence fees plus a contribution
toward Cardinal Foods' expedited freight on two missed outbound trailers. This
was a commercial decision to protect a 4.42 million USD annual relationship and a
2.8 million USD expansion pipeline. It was, in my assessment, correct and cheap.

It is explicitly not precedent, it is documented as such in the revised SLA
policy, and any future request to match it routes through me.

Indirect cost

  The Cardinal Foods Atlanta expansion decision, approximately 1.9 million USD,
  was expected to close in Q4 2024. The conversation did not resume until
  November and the decision is now expected in Q1 2025. That is roughly a
  two-month revenue deferral on the largest single item in the pipeline.

  I want to be precise about the causal claim: the customer did not pause the
  expansion as a punishment. Their attention was consumed by remediation and
  their internal confidence-rebuilding took time. But the delay is real, it is
  attributable, and it is larger than every direct cost combined.

Contract terms impact

  The revised SLA policy effective 2024-10-01 changed availability measurement to
  require dispatch eligibility rather than mere power, connection, and absence of
  faults. Under the old definition, the worst outage in company history measured
  as near-perfect availability, which was an untenable position with a customer
  who could do the arithmetic.

  The revision increases our credit exposure. I modelled it against the trailing
  twelve months of incidents and estimate incremental annual credit exposure of
  120,000 to 200,000 USD under the new definition. I recommended it anyway and
  the board endorsed it. An availability commitment that a customer does not
  believe is worth nothing commercially, and Sales now leads with the tightened
  definition as a differentiator against competitors whose definitions remain
  loose.

Cardinal Foods at 13.3 percent of ARR is our largest concentration risk, and
September made that concrete rather than theoretical. The mitigation is not to
reduce Cardinal Foods; it is to grow everything else, and specifically to unblock
the regulated segment.

Approximately 4.4 million USD of pipeline is currently gated on a single missing
feature — Operator Console single sign-on — of which 2.6 million USD is Voss
Pharma expansion into Frankfurt and Dublin. That is the highest-confidence
revenue unlock available to this company and it is scheduled for H1 2025. I have
asked that it be protected from reallocation, and the roadmap reflects that.
