Product line: HX-200 Courier — firmware 3.x
Maintainer: Tobias Lindqvist, Principal Engineer, Firmware
Last revised: 2024-12-05
Classification: Internal — Engineering. Customer-facing release notes are
                published separately and omit internal defect references.


VERSIONING AND SUPPORT POLICY

HX-200 firmware is versioned independently of Helix Fleet OS. Fleet OS release N
is supported against any firmware release from the preceding twelve months.
Since Q4 2024 that compatibility window is enforced by an automated cross-version
CI matrix rather than by a maintained document. Prior to November 2024 it was
documentation only, which is a contributing cause of INC-2024-017.

Firmware owns everything safety-critical and everything real-time: motor
control, emergency stop handling, obstacle detection, localization, and battery
management. Fleet OS cannot override these. Any firmware change touching motion
safety requires sign-off from Nikolai Petrov (Safety and Compliance) and may
require re-certification against ISO 3691-4.

Update channels:
  STABLE   — general availability. Default for all production sites.
  STAGED   — released to opt-in sites 14 days before STABLE promotion.
  LTS      — 3.6.x line, security and critical fixes only, supported until
             2026-06-30. Used by Voss Pharma Basel under their change-control
             requirements.

3.9.0  —  released 2024-12-02  (STABLE)
=============================================================================

Features
  - Adaptive obstacle clearance. Robots now modulate passing distance based on
    obstacle classification from Helix Vision rather than applying a fixed
    400 mm envelope. Reduces unnecessary stops in congested aisles by roughly
    30 percent in field trials at Trellis Home Goods.
  - Battery health telemetry expanded: per-cell voltage deviation and cumulative
    charge-cycle count now reported on the standard telemetry frame. Feeds the
    predictive battery replacement model requested by Field Operations.
  - Reduced cold-start localization time from 22 s to 9 s typical.

Fixes
  - Fixed rare localization drift in aisles with highly repetitive rack geometry.
    Affected Brightline Apparel Reno specifically; their rack layout is unusually
    uniform and defeated the previous scan-matching heuristic.
  - E-207 (LiDAR occlusion) no longer latches after the occlusion clears. Robots
    previously required a manual fault acknowledgement; they now self-clear after
    3 consecutive clean scans.
  - Corrected motor current reporting scale factor on units manufactured after
    2024-06 with the revised drive board.

Compatibility
  - Requires Fleet OS 4.2.0 or later. Verified against 4.2.0 through 4.2.3 in the
    cross-version CI matrix.

3.8.2  —  released 2024-09-17  (STABLE)  —  CRITICAL
=============================================================================

This release is one half of the fix for INC-2024-017. It MUST be deployed
together with Fleet OS 4.2.2 or later. Neither patch alone is sufficient — they
address different halves of the interaction.

Fixes
  - State-of-charge reporting now crosses configured threshold boundaries
    cleanly rather than latching inside the 0.5 percent hysteresis band
    introduced in 3.8.1. The hysteresis band is retained for display smoothing
    but is now bypassed when the underlying value crosses a threshold registered
    by Fleet OS.

    Background: in 3.8.1, a robot arriving at a dock near the 20 percent charge
    threshold could latch a reported SoC in the 20.0 to 20.5 percent range and
    hold it while physically charging to full. Fleet OS 4.2.1 compared reported
    SoC against the release target using a strict inequality and therefore never
    released the dock reservation. Because dock reservations had no expiry lease,
    the robot held the dock indefinitely. At Cardinal Foods Memphis this affected
    61 of 240 units and cost 6 hours 47 minutes of degraded throughput.

  - Added a diagnostic counter, soc_report_latch_events, exposed on telemetry.
    Any non-zero value on 3.8.2 or later indicates the latch condition was
    detected and bypassed, and should be reported to firmware engineering.

Upgrade urgency
  CRITICAL for any site running 3.8.0 or 3.8.1. All affected production sites
  were upgraded by 2024-09-30.


=============================================================================
3.8.1  —  released 2024-08-19  (STABLE)  —  SUPERSEDED, DO NOT DEPLOY
=============================================================================

WITHDRAWN. This release, in combination with Fleet OS 4.2.0 or 4.2.1, causes the
charge dock reservation deadlock documented in INC-2024-017. Any site still
running 3.8.1 must upgrade to 3.8.2 or later immediately.

Changes as originally released
  - Battery state-of-charge reporting changed from integer percentage to float,
    with a 0.5 percent hysteresis band applied to suppress display oscillation.
    The motivation was cosmetic: SoC readings visibly flickered between adjacent
    integer values on the Operator Console, and several customers had reported it
    as a defect.
  - Improved charge current regulation in the 80 to 100 percent region, reducing
    time to full charge by approximately 6 minutes.
  - Fixed spurious E-502 (battery thermal cutoff) reports on units operating in
    ambient temperatures above 32 C.

Post-incident note from Tobias Lindqvist
  The hysteresis change was reviewed, tested, and shipped correctly against
  Fleet OS 4.1.x, which was current at the time. It was never tested against
  4.2.x. The change was locally sound and its consequences were entirely outside
  the scope of what firmware testing could have been expected to catch in
  isolation. That is precisely the argument for the cross-version CI matrix,
  which is now in place. I want it on record that the defect here is process,
  not the individual change.

3.8.0  —  released 2024-07-29  (STABLE)
=============================================================================

Features
  - Support for the revised drive board (hardware revision C), shipping on units
    manufactured from 2024-06 onward.
  - Payload shift detection (E-118) added. Uses IMU signature analysis to detect
    load movement during transit and halt before the load is lost. Requested by
    Cardinal Foods after three tote spills in Q2 2024.
  - Charging dock handshake protocol v2, reducing failed dock attempts from
    approximately 2 percent to under 0.2 percent.

Fixes
  - Fixed a race in the e-stop reset path that could require two physical reset
    presses. Safety-reviewed by Nikolai Petrov; no certification impact, as the
    failure mode was fail-safe (robot remained stopped).
  - Corrected wheel odometry calibration drift on units with more than 4,000
    operating hours.


=============================================================================
3.7.2  —  released 2024-06-11  (STABLE)
=============================================================================

Fixes
  - Fixed Wi-Fi roaming handoff failure that could cause a robot to lose Fleet OS
    connectivity for 8 to 15 seconds when crossing between access points. Robots
    correctly held position during the outage, but the resulting task
    reassignment churn was disruptive at high fleet density. Reported by Cardinal
    Foods Memphis.
  - Reduced idle power draw by 11 percent, extending standby endurance.


=============================================================================
3.7.1  —  released 2024-05-20  (STABLE)
=============================================================================

Fixes
  - Fixed E-115 (e-stop engaged) failing to clear on the Operator Console after
    physical reset, on units with hardware revision A only.
  - Improved recovery from transient LiDAR occlusion caused by airborne dust,
    which had been generating nuisance E-207 faults at Cardinal Foods.


=============================================================================
3.7.0  —  released 2024-04-15  (STABLE)
=============================================================================

Features
  - Helix Vision integration. Stereo perception module support, enabling
    obstacle classification (person, pallet, debris, forklift) rather than
    undifferentiated occupancy. Requires the Helix Vision hardware add-on.
  - Dynamic speed envelope: robots reduce maximum speed automatically in zones
    flagged as mixed-traffic in the facility map. Certification-relevant; signed
    off by Nikolai Petrov and included in the ISO 3691-4 re-assessment.

Fixes
  - Fixed a localization failure when starting from a fully powered-off state in
    a location not matching the last known pose.


=============================================================================
3.6.4  —  released 2024-11-19  (LTS)
=============================================================================

LTS backport release. Security and critical fixes only.

Fixes
  - Backported the 3.8.2 state-of-charge threshold crossing fix. Although the
    3.6.x line predates the hysteresis band and is not vulnerable to
    INC-2024-017, the threshold-crossing logic was aligned for consistency at
    the request of Voss Pharma, whose change control process requires that all
    known-relevant fixes be applied or formally waived.
  - Patched a TLS certificate validation weakness in the telemetry upload path.
    No evidence of exploitation. Reported through the Helix responsible
    disclosure process.

Support
  3.6.x LTS is supported until 2026-06-30. Voss Pharma Basel is the only
  production site on this line.

KNOWN ISSUES — CURRENT ACROSS 3.9.0
=============================================================================

  - FW-1142: Charging dock handshake occasionally fails on docks with firmware
    older than dock-fw 1.4. Workaround is to update dock firmware. Affects two
    docks at Brightline Apparel Reno.
  - FW-1156: Helix Vision obstacle classification degrades below 200 lux.
    Facilities with dim aisle lighting see increased conservative stops. Trellis
    Home Goods Columbus is affected in two aisles. Lighting remediation is a
    facility-side change; documented in the site requirements.
  - FW-1163: Cumulative charge-cycle count resets if the battery management
    board is replaced without running the cycle-count transfer utility. Field
    technicians must follow the battery replacement procedure exactly.
  - FW-1171: On hardware revision A units only, cold-start localization can
    exceed 30 s in facilities with more than 60 percent rack occupancy. Revision
    A units represent under 8 percent of the deployed fleet and are being
    retired through normal replacement.
