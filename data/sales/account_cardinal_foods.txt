INC-2024-017 — COMMERCIAL RECORD

On Saturday 2024-09-14, 61 of 240 HX-200 units at Memphis entered a charge dock
reservation deadlock. Fleet throughput degraded for 6 hours 47 minutes, peaking
at 71 percent loss, during their Saturday inbound shift. Cardinal Foods missed
two outbound trailer departures and delayed approximately 4,100 units of
fulfillment.

The technical account is in the INC-2024-017 postmortem. This section records the
commercial consequences, which were more severe than the technical ones.

What made this bad commercially was not the outage itself. Ray Sandoval has lived
through worse from other vendors and said so. Three things did the damage:

  1. We did not detect it. Hollis Trent telephoned us. For 41 minutes our
     monitoring showed a perfectly healthy fleet while 61 robots sat useless.
     Denise Okafor's first question in the executive review was not "why did it
     break" but "why did my shift supervisor have to tell you."

  2. Under the availability definition in force at the time, the worst outage in
     our company history measured as near-perfect availability, because the
     deadlocked robots were powered, connected, and unfaulted. Curtis Lam did
     the arithmetic himself and asked, reasonably, what exactly we were
     committing to. We did not have a good answer.

  3. Recovery was manual and slow. Clearing 61 reservations one at a time took
     2 hours 20 minutes of Ray Sandoval's operators' time, during a shift they
     were already losing.

Response and remediation

  2024-09-14   Rowan Achebe telephoned Denise Okafor personally the same
               evening. This mattered.
  2024-09-16   Fleet OS 4.2.2 released.
  2024-09-17   Firmware 3.8.2 released.
  2024-09-18   Both deployed at Memphis. Confirmed resolved.
  2024-09-23   Redacted customer-facing postmortem delivered, three days inside
               the 5-business-day Enterprise commitment.
  2024-09-26   On-site executive review. Rowan Achebe, Dana Whitfield, Elena
               Vasquez attended in person. Dana walked Ray Sandoval and Amara
               Diallo through the actual root cause in technical detail — the
               hysteresis band, the strict comparison, the missing lease — rather
               than a sanitized version. Ray later told Priyanka Deshmukh this
               was the thing that rebuilt his confidence.
  2024-10-01   Revised SLA policy issued, changing availability measurement to
               require dispatch eligibility. Cardinal Foods was shown this
               before it was published.

Service credit

  Contractual formula figure: 31,800 USD
  Assessed and paid: 148,000 USD

  The difference is a negotiated goodwill credit approved by Rowan Achebe and
  Sam Delacroix, covering the full quarter's licence fees for Memphis plus a
  contribution toward Cardinal Foods' expedited freight costs on the two missed
  trailers. This was a commercial decision to protect a 4.42 million USD
  relationship and a 2.8 million USD expansion pipeline, not a contractual
  entitlement.

  It is explicitly not precedent. It is recorded in the SLA policy as a worked
  example with that caveat attached, and any future request to match it should
  route through the CFO.

Outcome

  The relationship survived and is arguably stronger. Denise Okafor's assessment,
  relayed through Elena Vasquez in November: "You broke it, you told us exactly
  how, you fixed it in four days, and you changed your own contract terms because
  of it. That is more than I get from most vendors."

  The Atlanta expansion conversation resumed in November 2024, roughly two months
  later than originally planned. That two-month delay is the real cost of the
  incident, and it is larger than the credit.
