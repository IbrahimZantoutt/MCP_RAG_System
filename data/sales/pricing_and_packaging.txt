1. HOW WE SELL

Helix sells hardware and software separately. Robots are purchased or leased;
Fleet OS is licensed per robot per month. Support tier is chosen independently of
licence and sets availability commitments and response times.

A typical deal therefore has three components:

    hardware (capex or lease) + Fleet OS licence + support tier

Deployment services (site assessment, installation, map creation, integration,
training) are quoted separately as a one-time professional services engagement.

Dock provisioning is not optional and must be quoted correctly. Recommended
ratios are one dock per four HX-200 units and one dock per three HX-450 units.
The Stacker's higher ratio reflects its longer charge time and shorter runtime.
Under-provisioning docks is the single most common site design error on Stacker
deployments; it produces chronic throughput shortfall that customers reasonably
blame on the robots. Do not discount docks out of a deal to hit a number.

4. SUPPORT TIERS

Full definitions are in the Support SLA and Escalation Policy. Commercially:

  STANDARD       99.0 percent availability, next-business-day critical response,
                 business hours, no named contact. Included at no additional
                 charge with the Professional licence.

  PROFESSIONAL   99.5 percent availability, 4-hour critical response 24x7,
                 named support engineer. Included with the Professional licence.

  ENTERPRISE     99.9 percent availability, 1-hour critical response 24x7,
                 named Technical Account Manager, quarterly business review,
                 24-hour on-site response for SEV-1 where remote resolution has
                 failed. Requires the Enterprise licence at 265 USD per robot
                 per month.

The Enterprise tier is where the availability commitment becomes commercially
serious. Note that as of the 2024-10-01 policy revision, availability requires
robots to be eligible for dispatch, not merely powered and unfaulted. This
change followed INC-2024-017, where 61 deadlocked robots at Cardinal Foods
measured as available under the old definition while doing no work at all.

Sales staff must understand this change, because it makes the Enterprise
commitment meaningfully harder to meet and it is a strong differentiator against
competitors whose availability definitions remain loose. Lead with it.

6. DISCOUNT AUTHORITY — INTERNAL

  Account Executive              up to 5 percent beyond volume band
  VP Sales (Elena Vasquez)       up to 12 percent beyond volume band
  CFO (Sam Delacroix)            up to 20 percent beyond volume band
  CEO                            beyond 20 percent

Floor: no deal below 38 percent gross margin without CFO approval. Company gross
margin was 51 percent in Q3 2024, which is below where a hardware-plus-software
business needs to be, and hardware bill of materials is the principal reason.
HX-200 hardware revision D targets a 14 percent BOM reduction in H2 2025. Until
that lands, hardware discounting directly erodes a margin we cannot afford to
erode.

Do not discount the software licence to protect hardware price. Recurring
software revenue at high margin is the entire long-term thesis of the business.
Trading it for a one-time hardware win is the worst trade available.

  Site assessment                            12,000 USD (credited against order)

  Facility map creation                      18,000 to 45,000 USD by site size
  WMS integration, standard adapter          22,000 USD

  Operator training                          4,500 USD per session
  Field technician certification             7,500 USD per cohort

Site assessment is not optional and should never be waived to accelerate a deal.
The assessment is where pallet quality, floor flatness, aisle widths, lighting
levels, Wi-Fi coverage, and dock siting get validated. Most deployment problems
we have had trace to something an assessment would have caught:

  - Brightline Apparel Reno: unusually uniform rack geometry defeating scan
    matching, requiring a firmware fix in 3.9.0
  - Trellis Home Goods Columbus: two aisles below 200 lux, degrading Helix
    Vision classification
  - Multiple freezer proposals sized without accounting for the HX-450
    cold-chain runtime penalty (approximately 4.5 hours at -25 C rather than
    7 hours, with charge time extended by roughly 40 percent)


8. WHAT WE DO NOT SELL

Consistency matters here; these come up regularly.

  - Outdoor or yard operation. The HX-200 is IP54 and certified for indoor
    mixed-traffic use only. Not a configuration option, not a roadmap item.
  - High-bay racking. Neither product reaches above 2,400 mm. The HX-700 concept
    is a feasibility study with a July 2025 decision gate and must not be
    represented to customers as planned. Scope deals to what we can service.
  - Cloud-hosted Fleet OS. Deliberate architectural commitment, not a gap.
  - Multi-tenant Fleet OS. One instance per facility.
  - Automatic warm-standby failover. Manual by design, for safety reasons.
  - Third-party robot support in Fleet OS.
  - Raising the HX-450 mast-extended speed limit above 0.3 m/s.

The correct posture on all of these is a clear no with the reason, not a vague
"not currently." Prospects respect the former and lose trust over the latter.
